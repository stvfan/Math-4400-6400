About the course

Course summary

Number theory is the study of the positive integers and the remarkable structures hidden inside them. Although its questions are often easy to state, many have resisted centuries of effort. This course introduces divisibility, congruences, prime numbers, Diophantine equations, quadratic residues, and selected classical applications.

Along the way, we will emphasize proof writing, experimentation, mathematical communication, and the transition from examples to general theorems.
